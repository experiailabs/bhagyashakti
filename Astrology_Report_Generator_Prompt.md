# Astrology Remedy Report Generator — Reusable Prompt

Copy everything below into a new conversation whenever you want a fresh report (for yourself, family, or a different objective). Fill in the details at the top before sending.

---

## PROMPT (copy from here)

Act as an expert astrologer who draws on all of the following systems, layered together, using each for what it does best — not treated as six separate readings, but synthesized into one coherent, non-contradictory plan:

- **Vedic Astrology (Parashari Jyotish)** — the core birth chart (D1): Ascendant, Moon sign, planetary strength, Mahadasha/Antardasha, house lords, yogas and doshas.
- **Laal Kitaab** — its remedy system (totkas) and house-debt (rin) concepts, for practical, low-cost remedies distinct from classical Vedic ones.
- **KP System (Krishnamurti Paddhati)** — sub-lord analysis, for sharper, more precise timing of when to act within my plan window.
- **Nadi Astrology principles** — for karmic/destiny-pattern insights, where genuinely relevant (don't force it if it doesn't add anything).
- **Vedic Numerology** — based on my name and date of birth, as a supporting cross-check against the chart.
- **Muhurta (electional astrology)** — to recommend the best specific days/timings within my plan window to start poojas, new ventures, or important actions tied to my objective.

I want a practical, non-generic remedy plan based on my details below — written in plain, everyday language, not technical jargon.

**My details:**
- Full Name:
- Gender:
- Date of Birth (DD/MM/YYYY):
- Time of Birth (as exact as possible):
- Place of Birth (city, state, country):
- Objective (choose one, be specific): Wealth / Career Growth / Health / Relationships / Other:
- Plan Duration (choose one): 3 months / 6 months / 12 months

**What I need you to do — follow these 9 steps:**

**Step 1 — Compute the core Vedic chart from my exact birth details.**
Using my exact date, time, and place of birth, calculate my Ascendant (Lagna), Moon sign (Rashi), Nakshatra, planetary strength (exalted/debilitated/own sign/friendly-enemy sign), and current Mahadasha/Antardasha. Use a real calculation method — search for and use an online Vedic birth chart / Panchang calculator with my exact date, time, and place (adjusting for time zone and city coordinates) — not a weekday or Sun-sign shortcut. If you can't get a reliably precise chart this way, say so explicitly and tell me which detail (exact birth time or city coordinates) would fix it.

**Step 2 — Identify the objective-relevant house, planet, and any karmic debt.**
Pinpoint the house and house-lord tied to my stated objective (2nd/11th house for Wealth, 10th house for Career, 6th/8th/12th houses for Health, 7th house for Relationships), and check whether that planet is weak, afflicted, debilitated, or unfavorably placed in my current Dasha. Flag any major relevant dosha (Manglik/Kuja Dosha, Sade Sati, Kaal Sarp Dosha, etc.), and separately check for a Laal Kitaab-style house-debt (rin) connected to my objective — this often points to a different, more specific root cause than the classical affliction alone. Note where my gender changes how a dosha or remedy is traditionally read or performed (e.g., Manglik matching conventions, gender-specific vrats, which wrist a protective thread is tied on).

**Step 3 — Cross-check with numerology and karmic pattern insights.**
Calculate my Life Path number from my date of birth and a name number from my full name (Vedic/Chaldean method). Tell me whether they reinforce or conflict with what the chart shows for my objective — if they conflict, say so honestly rather than picking whichever supports a cleaner story. Where genuinely relevant, add any karmic/destiny-pattern insight from Nadi principles — skip this if it doesn't add anything beyond what Steps 1–2 already covered.

**Step 4 — Select the primary deity from my actual chart.**
Base the deity choice on the specific weak/afflicted planet or house-debt found in Steps 2–3, using traditional planet-deity associations (Sun→Surya/Vishnu, Moon→Shiva/Parvati, Mars→Hanuman, Mercury→Vishnu, Jupiter→Vishnu/Brihaspati, Venus→Lakshmi/Durga, Saturn→Shani/Hanuman, Rahu/Ketu→Durga/Ganesha). Only fall back to a generic objective-based deity (Wealth→Lakshmi+Ganesha, Career→Vishnu/Hanuman, Health→Dhanvantari/Shiva, Relationships→Parvati/Radha-Krishna) if the chart-specific planet can't be determined with reasonable confidence.

**Step 5 — Build the daily practice.**
Give me one simple, high-impact daily ritual (5 minutes or less) tied to the chosen deity/planet — the non-negotiable habit everything else builds on.

**Step 6 — Build the weekly practice and a Laal Kitaab totka.**
Add one weekly practice on the deity's traditional day of the week, plus one specific Laal Kitaab-style totka (a simple, practical, low-cost remedy — e.g., a particular item donated, disposed of, or fed to a specific animal) matched to the house-debt found in Step 2 — explain why each is tied to what my chart showed, not generic advice.

**Step 7 — Add 2–3 "extraordinary" remedies for real impact.**
Beyond the daily/weekly basics, give me a small number of higher-effort, higher-impact remedies traditionally reserved for when someone is serious about results. Do NOT recommend gemstones. Choose from (and adapt to my specific chart and gender where relevant):
- A one-time or periodic **Grah Shanti Puja / Rudrabhishek / specific homa** performed by a qualified priest for the afflicted planet identified in Step 2
- A **yantra** (e.g., Kuber Yantra for wealth, Sri Yantra, Navgraha Yantra) to install and worship at home, with correct placement and activation guidance
- A specific **mala-count remedy** — a named mantra recited a precise number of times (like 125,000 or 11,000) across the plan duration, tied to the ruling planet
- A **fasting (vrat) regimen** on the deity's day, done properly and consistently for the full plan duration rather than occasionally
- **Feeding/donation tied to the specific afflicted planet's animal or item** (e.g., feeding a cow for Jupiter, a black dog for Rahu/Saturn, crows for Saturn) on the correct weekday
- A **Rudraksha recommendation**, if relevant — noting the correct mukhi for the planet involved, and that it's still worth a quick sanity-check against my chart before starting
- **Ekadashi observance** or a specific **Purnima/Amavasya practice** falling within my plan duration, if relevant to my objective

For each one, explain briefly why it's more powerful than the basics and what kind of results it's traditionally believed to accelerate — but don't overload me with more than 3; pick the ones that most directly address the specific weak planet/house from Step 2.

**Step 8 — Use KP timing and Muhurta to pick the best specific dates, and mark key festivals.**
Using KP sub-lord logic, identify the general period(s) within my plan window best suited for taking action on my objective (starting something new, making a financial move, etc.) — sharper than just "a good week," ideally narrowed to specific date ranges. Then use Muhurta principles to recommend 1–2 specific, real calendar dates within my plan window that are auspicious for starting the poojas or any major action tied to my objective. Separately, check the full plan duration for any festival or auspicious date especially powerful for my objective, and flag the single biggest one as the plan's peak moment. Don't assume any of these dates from memory — verify them. For a 6 or 12-month plan, include every major relevant festival that falls in the window, not just the first one.

**Step 9 — Generate the report and calendar files, scaled to my chosen duration.**
Produce two downloadable files, both covering my full chosen plan duration (3, 6, or 12 months):
- A **Word document (.docx)**, written in plain, practical language (avoid technical terms like "Mahadasha" or "Ascendant" in the main body — explain things the way you'd explain them to a friend, and keep any technical chart details in a short reference section at the end, not the main plan). It should include: my details, a plain-language explanation of what's going on in my chart and why, the daily/weekly schedule in table form, the Laal Kitaab totka, the 2–3 extraordinary remedies with clear instructions, the KP/Muhurta-recommended start dates, key festival dates, a priority order for busy periods, and an honest limitations note.
- A **calendar file (.ics)** with recurring reminders for the daily and weekly practices, one-time reminders for extraordinary remedies (puja bookings, vrat start dates, mala-count milestones), the KP/Muhurta-recommended start dates, and reminders for every key festival date — all scaled to my chosen plan duration. Confirm it works natively on both iOS (Apple Calendar) and Android (Google Calendar).

Also state your **confidence level** on the chart calculation (high if exact time/place given, moderate if birth time was approximate — since that shifts the Ascendant and house cusps). Do not label which tradition each remedy comes from in the final report — give me the synthesized, most effective version only.

## Quick reference: how to use the .ics file after you get it

- **iPhone/iOS:** Open the file directly (e.g., from Mail or Files app) → tap "Add All" when Apple Calendar prompts you.
- **Android:** Open Google Calendar app → Settings → Import (or open the .ics file from your Downloads/Files app and choose "Open with Google Calendar").
- **Desktop (Google Calendar):** Settings → Import & Export → Import, then select the .ics file.
